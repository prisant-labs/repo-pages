# Initech Intranet

Welcome to the Initech intranet. Everything a new hire needs is in here somewhere, usually one folder deeper than you expect.

> This is sample content for the repo-pages demo. Initech, its people and its memos are a parody of a 1999 film about office life. No real company is described.

## Folders

- **handbook/** - policies, benefits and the dress code.
- **memos/** - management memos, newest first. Drafts that were never sent live in `memos/_drafts/`.
- **projects/** - active projects, each with its own README.
- **teams/** - team pages: software, accounting, facilities.
- **data/** - CSV, JSON and YAML exports from various systems.
- **forms/** - the TPS cover sheet and the supply requisition form.
- **media/** - logos, diagrams and the floor plan.
- **neighbors/** - Chotchkie's, the restaurant across the parking lot.
- **_archive/** - superseded documents. Kept because nobody is sure who is allowed to delete them.

## House rules for this wiki

1. Every document needs front matter with a `description` and `tags`.
2. Link to other pages with relative links, so they survive a move.
3. If you rename a page, the old link still works. The site keeps a rename map.
4. Do not move the stapler. See [office supplies](handbook/policies/office-supplies.md).

See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for the full process.
