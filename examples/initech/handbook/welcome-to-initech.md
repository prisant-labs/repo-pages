# Welcome to Initech

Congratulations on joining Initech, a leader in software for the banking industry since well before you were hired.

## Your first day

1. **Badge.** Nina at the front desk has it. She is usually on the phone. Wait.
2. **Cubicle.** Your cubicle number is on your offer letter. The walls are 62 inches tall, chosen after a study.
3. **Computer.** It is already set up. It is set up for the last person. Ask Michael.
4. **Coffee.** The machine on the fourth floor works. The one on the third floor makes a noise but also works.

## Your first week

- Read the [TPS reports policy](policies/tps-reports.md). You will be asked about it.
- Learn the [cover sheet](../forms/tps-cover-sheet.pdf). Practice attaching it.
- Meet your managers. Plural. See the [org chart](org-chart.md).
- Find out where the [stapler](policies/office-supplies.md) lives. Do not take it.
