# Birthday cake

Each month, everyone with a birthday that month shares one cake.

## Distribution

1. The cake is placed in the break room at 3:00 PM.
2. Pieces are passed down the line, hand to hand.
3. Plates run out before the line does. Order more plates.

## Known issues

| Issue | Status |
|---|---|
| The last person in line does not get cake | Open, reported several times |
| Cake is ordered for the wrong month | Fixed |
| Someone's name is spelled wrong on the cake | Open |

If you did not get cake, let Human Resources know. They will note it.
