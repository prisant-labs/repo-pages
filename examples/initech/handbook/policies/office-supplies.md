# Office supplies

## Requesting supplies

Use the [requisition form](../../forms/requisition-form.html). Facilities fills orders on Tuesdays.

| Item | Limit | Approval |
|---|---|---|
| Pens | 1 box a quarter | None |
| Paper | As needed | None |
| Toner | 1 cartridge | Michael, after he has checked the printer |
| Staplers | 1 per desk, ever | Facilities manager |

## Staplers

The company switched stapler brands to save money. The new staplers jam. Some employees kept their old staplers. This is allowed as long as the stapler is personal property and stays at the employee's own desk.

If a red stapler goes missing, do not take matters into your own hands. File a requisition. See the [stapler requisitions](../../data/stapler-requisitions.csv) for how that has gone so far.

## Moving supplies

Supplies move with your desk when you are relocated. Your stapler moves with you. See [desk relocation](desk-relocation.md).
