# Saturday work

Saturday work is voluntary. You will be asked about it on Friday afternoon, in person, while you are packing up.

## How it works

1. A manager stops by your cubicle holding a coffee mug.
2. The manager mentions that the team is a little behind.
3. The manager wonders aloud whether you could come in tomorrow.
4. The manager mentions Sunday, too.

## Pay

Salaried employees do not get extra pay for Saturday. They get the gratitude of their managers, expressed in a memo.

## Opting out

There is no form for this. People have tried saying they will be busy. See [the memo on Saturday coverage](../../memos/from-bill-lumbergh/saturday-coverage.md).
