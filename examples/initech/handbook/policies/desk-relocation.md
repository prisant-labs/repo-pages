# Desk relocation

Facilities may move your desk to make room for a new team, a printer, or a consultant.

## Notice

You will be told when it is happening, or shortly afterwards.

## Locations, in order of preference

1. Your current floor
2. Another floor
3. Storage room B, in the basement. See [the facilities page](../../teams/facilities/basement-storage-room-b.md).

## Your things

Your stapler goes with you. Your radio goes with you, at a low volume. Your desk lamp goes with you if it was yours.

If you are moved to the basement, Facilities will make sure the lights work.
