# Parking

## Lots

| Lot | Who | Notes |
|---|---|---|

| C, back | Overflow | Near the dumpsters. Bring a jacket |

## Getting here

The freeway backs up between 7:40 and 8:20. The side road beside it moves about as fast as you can walk. People who switch lanes do not arrive sooner; we have checked.

## Construction

The building across the lot is being renovated. Construction noise starts at 7:00. Facilities has been asked to ask the contractor to start at 7:30.
