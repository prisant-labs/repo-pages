# Efficiency review kickoff

**From:** Bob Slydell and Bob Porter, consultants
**To:** All staff

Hi gang!

We are the Bobs. We have been brought in to help Initech work smarter. Over the next two weeks we will meet with each of you for a short, friendly conversation.

## What we will ask

1. Walk us through a typical day.
2. What would you say you do here?
3. If you had to cut one thing from your week, what would it be?

## What we will not do

- We will not make any decisions in the interviews.
- We will not write anything down in front of you. We will write it down afterwards.

## Schedule

See the [interview schedule](interview-schedule.csv). If your slot does not work, tell Nina. She will tell us.

Looking forward to it!

Bob and Bob
