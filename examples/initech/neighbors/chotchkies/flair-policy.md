# Flair policy

This is Chotchkie's policy, posted here because Initech staff keep asking.

## The rule

Servers wear a minimum of fifteen pieces of flair.

## The other rule

The minimum is the minimum. Management has expressed a preference for servers who want to express themselves beyond it.

| Pieces of flair | What management thinks |
|---|---|
| Fewer than 15 | Not allowed |
| Exactly 15 | Allowed. Noticed |
| 37 | An example to others |

## Advice from Initech staff

If the expectation is more than the minimum, it would help to make that the minimum. This feedback has been given.
