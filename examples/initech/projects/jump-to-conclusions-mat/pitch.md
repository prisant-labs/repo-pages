# The Jump to Conclusions Mat

**From:** Tom Smykowski
**Status:** side project, not approved by Initech

## The idea

A mat. On the floor. It has different conclusions printed on it, and you jump to one. That is the whole idea. It is a mat, and you jump to conclusions.

Try the [interactive demo](jump-to-conclusions-mat.html). There is a [layout drawing](mat-layout.svg) too.

## Why it will sell

| Reason | Evidence |
|---|---|
| People jump to conclusions every day | The office |
| Nobody has a mat for it | Tom checked |
| Fun for the whole family | Tom's family, mainly |
