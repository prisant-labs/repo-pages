# Initech employee handbook (1998)

> Superseded. See the [current handbook](../handbook/README.md).

## Section 1: Hours

Core hours are 8:00 to 5:00. Lunch is 30 minutes. Chotchkie's has not opened yet.

## Section 2: Reports

TPS reports are filed monthly. There is one cover sheet. It is fine.

## Section 3: Staplers

Every desk gets one stapler. Any brand. Nobody minds.

## Section 4: The year 2000

It is a long way off. Not a priority.
