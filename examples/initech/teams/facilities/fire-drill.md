# Fire drill

Updated after the fire. The fire was real. This page is about drills.

## Procedure

1. Leave by the nearest stairway. Not the elevator.
2. Do not go back for your TPS reports. They are backed up.
3. Do not go back for your stapler.
4. Meet in the far corner of lot B.
